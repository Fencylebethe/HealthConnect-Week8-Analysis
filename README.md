## Final Headline Finding

Booking lead time and previous no-show history compound to create a **nearly 3x difference** in no-show risk:

| Risk Profile | No-Show Rate |
|---|---|
| Best case (0-7 days lead time, low no-show history) | 26% |
| Worst case (31+ days lead time, high no-show history) | 73% |

This finding was independently validated in Week 7 and confirmed to hold consistently across gender.

## Final Recommendation

HealthConnect Clinic should implement a **combined risk score** (booking lead time × previous no-show history) to prioritise reminder intensity — directing intensive outreach (e.g., confirmation calls) to the highest-risk patients, and standard reminders to the lowest-risk patients.
